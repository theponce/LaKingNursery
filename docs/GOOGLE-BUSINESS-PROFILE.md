## 1. Claiming or Creating the Listing

### Check if a listing already exists

1. Open Google Maps at https://maps.google.com
2. Search for **"La King Nursery Kingsville TX"**
3. If a listing appears, check whether it says **"Own this business?"** or **"Claim this business"** — that means it is unclaimed.
4. If no listing appears, you will create one from scratch.

### Claim an existing listing

5. Click **"Own this business?"** or **"Claim this business"** on the listing.
6. Sign in with the Google account you want to manage the business from (ideally a dedicated business Gmail, not a personal one).
7. Follow the prompts. Google will ask you to verify ownership.

### Create a new listing (if none exists)

8. Go to https://business.google.com
9. Click **"Add your business to Google"**
10. Enter the business name: **La King Nursery**
11. Follow the step-by-step wizard (details in Section 2 below).

### Verification options

12. Google will offer one or more verification methods. The most common are:
    - **Postcard by mail** — Google sends a postcard with a PIN to 1304 E Kennedy, Kingsville, TX 78363. Takes 5-14 days.
    - **Phone call or text** — Instant PIN sent to (361) 228-5193 if this option is available.
    - **Email** — Sent to an email associated with the business domain.
    - **Instant verification via Google Search Console** — If lakingnursery.com is already verified in Search Console under the same Google account, verification may be instant.
13. Choose whichever method is available. Phone/text is fastest. If only postcard is offered, request it and continue setting up the profile while waiting — most edits will go live after verification.

## 6. Photos Strategy

Photos are one of the highest-impact elements of a GBP listing. Businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for directions and 35% more website clicks (per Google's own data).

### Required photos (upload as soon as available)

48. **Logo** (profile photo)
    - The La King Nursery logo on a clean background
    - Recommended: 720 x 720 px, square crop, PNG or JPG
    - This appears in search results and on Google Maps

49. **Cover photo**
    - A wide shot of the nursery storefront or a beautiful display of plants
    - Recommended: 1080 x 608 px (16:9 ratio)
    - This is the first thing people see when they find the listing

### Additional photos to gather and upload over time

50. **Exterior photos** (2-3)
    - Front of the building, signage, parking area
    - Helps customers recognize the location when arriving

51. **Interior photos** (3-5)
    - Plant displays, aisles, greenhouse areas, supply shelves
    - Shows the breadth of inventory

52. **Product photos** (5-10)
    - Close-ups of popular plants, trees, shrubs, seasonal color
    - Bagged soil and mulch, garden supplies
    - Update seasonally with new arrivals

53. **Team photos** (2-3)
    - Staff helping customers, working on a landscape project, loading plants
    - Builds trust and shows the human side of the business

54. **Work/project photos** (5-10)
    - Before and after landscape installations
    - Completed hardscaping projects (patios, walkways)
    - Lawn care transformations
    - Tree trimming results
    - These serve as a visual portfolio

55. **Seasonal updates**
    - Upload new photos monthly or at least seasonally
    - Spring blooms, fall color, holiday displays, new shipments
    - Fresh photos signal to Google that the business is active

### Photo guidelines

56. All photos should be:
    - Minimum 720 px on the shortest side (Google's requirement)
    - Well-lit, in focus, not heavily filtered
    - JPG or PNG format, under 5 MB each
    - Geotagged if possible (taken on a phone at the nursery location)
    - No watermarks, logos overlaid on photos, or promotional text

**Note:** Photos are being gathered and can be uploaded incrementally. Start with the logo and cover photo, then add others as they become available. Aim for at least 15-20 photos within the first month.

## 8. Posts Strategy

Google Business Profile posts appear directly on your listing in search results. They expire after 7 days (except Event and Offer posts, which expire on their end date), so consistent posting is important.

### Post types available

- **What's New** — General updates, tips, and news. Best for weekly engagement.
- **Offer** — Promotions and deals with start/end dates and optional coupon codes.
- **Event** — Events with a title, date range, and details.

### Posting cadence

62. Post **once per week** at minimum. Aim for every Monday or Tuesday morning.
63. Rotate between the post types to keep the listing fresh.

## 9. Review Strategy

Reviews are the single most influential factor in local search ranking and customer trust. The goal is a steady, authentic stream of reviews — not a sudden burst.

### Generate your review link

72. Go to https://business.google.com and select the La King Nursery listing.
73. Click **"Ask for reviews"** (or look under "Get more reviews"). Google will provide a short link.
74. The link format is typically: **https://g.page/r/YOUR-PLACE-ID/review**
75. Save this link. You will use it everywhere.

### How to ask for reviews

76. **In person (most effective):** After completing a service or helping a customer in the nursery, say something like:
    > "We are glad we could help. If you have a minute, a Google review really helps other folks in Kingsville find us. I can text you the link if you would like."

77. **Text message:** Send the review link via text to customers after completing a project. Keep the message short:
    > "Hi [Name], thanks for choosing La King Nursery for your [service]. If you were happy with the work, we would really appreciate a quick Google review — it helps us a lot: [REVIEW LINK]"

78. **Email:** Include the review link in project completion emails or follow-up communications.

79. **In-store signage:** Print a small sign or card with a QR code linking to the review page. Place it near the register and on the service counter.

80. **On receipts or business cards:** Add "Leave us a Google review" with the short link or QR code.

### Review response templates

Respond to every review — positive and negative — within 24-48 hours. Responses show future customers that you are engaged and care.

**For positive reviews (5 stars):**

> Thank you, [Name]! We are glad we could help with your [mention what they referenced if specific, e.g., "landscape project" or "plant selection"]. It is always great to see South Texas yards thriving. We appreciate you taking the time to share your experience, and we look forward to seeing you at the nursery again.

**For positive reviews (4 stars):**

> Thank you for the kind words, [Name]. We are happy you had a good experience. If there is anything we can do even better next time, we are always open to feedback. We appreciate your support.

**For negative reviews (constructive):**

> [Name], thank you for sharing your feedback. We are sorry your experience did not meet your expectations, and we take this seriously. We would like the chance to make it right. Please call us at (361) 228-5193 or stop by the nursery so we can discuss how to resolve this.

**For negative reviews (vague or unfair):**

> Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We strive to provide excellent service to every customer. We are not able to find details about your experience, but we would like to learn more. Please reach out to us at (361) 228-5193 so we can look into this.

### Review guidelines

81. **Never** offer discounts, free products, or incentives in exchange for reviews. This violates Google's policies and can result in your listing being penalized.
82. **Never** ask only satisfied customers. A natural mix of ratings (with a high average) looks more authentic than a wall of 5-star reviews.
83. Aim for **2-4 new reviews per month** as a sustainable pace.
84. If you notice a fake or spam review, report it through the GBP dashboard by clicking the three-dot menu on the review and selecting "Report review."
